Privacy and terms

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Ripple does not collect, keep, or use your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Privacy

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings: the folder you rip into, your format and quality, and a few switches. They are saved in Ripple’s preferences on your Mac and nowhere else.
  • Your music files and the album details you type. They go where you tell Ripple to put them.
  • BradysBit never receives any of it.

What leaves your Mac

Only the album lookup sends anything over the internet, and only when you put in a disc. Ripple asks three public services for details about the disc. Your Mac talks to them directly. BradysBit does not see or keep these requests.

  • MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) is told the disc’s ID and where each of its tracks starts. That is a fingerprint of the disc, not of you. It holds no file names and no personal details.
  • The Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org, whose pictures are hosted by archive.org) is asked for the cover of an album MusicBrainz found.
  • Apple’s iTunes Search (itunes.apple.com) is asked for a cover by artist and album title, only when the Cover Art Archive has none.

Like any website, these services can see your internet address when your Mac connects to them, and they follow their own privacy policies. If you are offline, Ripple still rips your disc. You just type the album details yourself.

Other things worth knowing

  • To offer to clear away its own installer, Ripple looks at which disk images are open on your Mac. That stays on your Mac.
  • This web page loads nothing from other sites: no fonts, scripts, counters, or trackers. The web host may keep ordinary server logs, as web servers do. If you follow the Ko-fi link, Ko-fi’s privacy policy applies there.
  • If macOS offers to send a crash report to Apple, that is your choice in macOS, and it goes to Apple, not to BradysBit.
  • If any of this changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.

Terms

  • Ripple is free and comes as is. There is no promise that it will work for you or that it has no mistakes. Use it at your own risk. Discs can be scratched or misread, and burns can fail, so keep your originals and check your results.
  • Use it only with music you own or have the right to copy. You are responsible for following the copyright laws where you live.
  • Other people’s parts. Ripple includes LAME, under the GNU LGPL, as a separate library you can replace. Album details come from MusicBrainz, and cover pictures come from the Cover Art Archive and Apple. They belong to their owners and have their own terms.
  • Tips are voluntary gifts. A tip on Ko-fi is not a payment for anything and does not buy extra features or support.
  • You can stop using Ripple at any time by deleting it.
